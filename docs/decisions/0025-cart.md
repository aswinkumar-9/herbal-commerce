# 0025. Cart

- Status: Accepted and implemented (part 6)
- Date: 2026-11-06

## Context

Part 5 added stock (ADR 0024). Next on the MVP list (`docs/requirements.md`, section 6) come the cart, checkout, delivery charges and tax, and order confirmation. Part 6 builds the cart; checkout and orders follow.

The permission matrix (section 4) gives the cart to its customer alone: "own only". Staff have no part in it.

The owner made four choices on 2026-11-06:
- **A cart on the server for signed-in customers only.** A guest's picks stay on the device, in the app or on the website, until the guest signs in.
- **Stock is held when the order is placed,** never by the cart.
- **Many different products, but at most 4 packs of one product, all its pack sizes together.** The owner first answered "no limit" for a single pack size, then set this rule for the product.
- **A cart nobody changes for 90 days is deleted.**

## Decision

### The tables

- **`carts`:** one per customer.
  - `userId`, unique, with a foreign key `ON DELETE CASCADE`: the cart goes with its customer;
  - `createdAt`;
  - `updatedAt`: the last change to the cart or to its lines, indexed for the cleanup.

  A cart is made when its first pack goes in.
- **`cart_items`:** one pack size in a cart.
  - `variantId`, with a foreign key `ON DELETE CASCADE`, and an index;
  - `packs`: how many packs;
  - `createdAt` and `updatedAt`.

  Each pack size is in a cart once. A line keeps no price.

**Both foreign keys cascade.** A pack size or product deleted by a Super User leaves the carts quietly, and none of the cart's keys answers `P2003`. So the mappings of ADR 0024, where `P2003` from a pack size or product means "still has stock", stay right.

### The limits

| Limit | Why |
|---|---|
| 1 to 4 packs in a line | No line can go over the product's limit on its own |
| **4 packs of one product, all its pack sizes together** | The owner's rule. It can't be dodged by mixing sizes: with 3 packs of 60 capsules, only 1 pack of 120 capsules fits. Other products don't count. |
| 50 pack sizes in a cart | A guard, so that no cart grows without end. The owner wants many products in a cart, and 50 leaves plenty of room. |

Two layers enforce them, as for a live product's details (ADR 0021):
1. **The API** checks every change after locking the cart, in `packsRefusal` (`cart/cart-rules.ts`). The answer is a clear `409`, such as `At most 4 packs of one product, all its pack sizes together. The cart already has 3 in other sizes.`
2. **The database** has a check constraint, `cart_items_packs_chk`, for 1 to 4 packs, and a trigger, `cart_items_limits`, for the other two:
   - the trigger runs **after** the line is written, so it counts the new line too;
   - it first locks the cart's row with `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, so two changes to one cart take turns.

   Why not `FOR UPDATE`: writing a line also makes its foreign key take a small lock on the cart. `FOR UPDATE` would wait for that lock, so two lines added at once could wait for each other forever. `NO KEY UPDATE` doesn't wait for it (ADR 0003).

The database rules were tested in 10 cases, on temporary tables inside a transaction that was rolled back, before the migration was applied.

### Changing the cart

| Endpoint | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/me/cart` | The cart. A customer without one gets an empty cart. |
| `PUT /v1/me/cart/items/:sku` | Sets how many packs of the pack size the cart holds, 1 to 4: adds it, or changes its packs. The answer is the whole cart. |
| `DELETE /v1/me/cart/items/:sku` | Takes the pack size out, sold now or not |
| `DELETE /v1/me/cart` | Empties the cart: the cart goes, with its lines |

- **A pack size is named by its SKU,** exactly as a product's page shows it. The shop shows no IDs (ADR 0021, ADR 0022), and the SKU is unique already.
- **Only a pack size sold now goes in:** on sale, in a live product of an active category. Any other answers `404 Pack size not found`, like an unknown SKU, as in the shop.
- **`PUT` sets a number; it doesn't add one.** Sending `{"packs":2}` twice still means 2 packs, so a request retried on a poor mobile connection does no harm.
- **Adding needs stock:** a sellable batch (ADR 0024). More packs of a pack size out of stock answer `409 This pack size is out of stock.`
- **Fewer packs are always fine,** even out of stock, so a customer can always tidy the cart. A pack size no longer sold can only be removed.
- **One change at a time per cart.** One statement makes or finds the cart, marks it as changed and locks it until the transaction ends:

  ```sql
  INSERT … ON CONFLICT ("userId") DO UPDATE SET "updatedAt" = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP RETURNING id
  ```

  Two first packs at the same moment still make one cart. A refused change rolls everything back, so it never leaves an empty cart behind.
- **Removing a pack size marks the cart as changed too.** Emptying deletes the cart.

### What the cart shows

- **Each line, the first added first:**
  - the product's name, slug and main photo;
  - the pack size as on the product's page: SKU, size, MRP and price;
  - `packs`, and `lineTotalRupees`;
  - `status`: `AVAILABLE` (on sale and in stock), `OUT_OF_STOCK`, or `OFF_SALE` (taken off sale, or its product or category hidden).
- **The totals count only the `AVAILABLE` lines:** `packs`, `subtotalRupees` and `mrpTotalRupees`. The other lines stay in the cart until the customer removes them, so nothing disappears without the customer seeing why.
- **Prices are always today's.** The cart never stores one, so a price change shows at once, and no client can ever set one. The order will fix the price when it is placed.
- **No IDs, and no stock counts:** only each line's status, as in the shop (ADR 0024).

### Modules ask each other

The cart reads only its own tables (ADR 0001):
- **the catalog** describes pack sizes through `ShopPacksService` (`findBySku`, `findByIds`): the pack, its product's name, slug and photo, whether it is sold now, and every pack size of its product, for the limit. The catalog alone decides what "sold now" means, the same rule as the shop;
- **the inventory** says which pack sizes are in stock, through `StockService.inStock()`.

### Who may do what

| | Visitor | Customer | Admin | Super User |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keep a cart | No | Their own only | No | No |
| See or change someone else's cart | No | No | No | No |

**The cart has no ID:** it is always the one of the customer in the access token, so no customer can reach another's (BOLA). There are no staff routes for carts, and a staff token gets `401` on the customer's routes.

### Audit and cleanup

- **Cart changes are not in the audit log.** It records staff actions, and a customer's own changes never go there, like their addresses (ADR 0019). A cart becomes a business record only as an order.
- **A cart nobody changes for 90 days is deleted,** with its lines, by the hourly cleanup (ADR 0011, `RETENTION.idleCartDays`). Looking at a cart doesn't count as a change. A cart changed at the very moment the cleanup runs is kept: the database checks `updatedAt` again before deleting it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Carts for guests too,** with an anonymous token. Rejected by the owner: more to secure and to clean up. The app or the website keeps a guest's picks and sends them after sign-in.
- **Holding stock in the cart.** Rejected by the owner: customers who browse but never buy would keep stock from those who do, and every hold would need an expiry.
- **A limit per pack size, or no limit.** Rejected by the owner: a limit per pack size is dodged by mixing sizes, and no limit lets one buyer empty a small batch (no dealers, decision D1).
- **Keeping carts 30 days, or until emptied.** Rejected by the owner: 30 days is too short for a customer who comes back next month, and forever keeps data with no end date (DPDP Act).
- **Storing the price in the line.** Rejected: the cart would show an old price, and the price must be fixed only when the order is placed.
- **A `POST` that adds packs to the count.** Rejected: a retried request would add them twice.
- **Lines by ID** (`/items/:id`). Rejected: the shop shows no IDs, and the SKU is unique already.
- **Reading the catalog's and the inventory's tables from the cart.** Rejected (ADR 0001).
- **Checking the limits in the API only.** Rejected, as in ADR 0021: a bug or a manual SQL change could break them.
- **A lock with `FOR UPDATE` in the trigger.** Rejected: two lines added at once could deadlock (above).

## Consequences

- **Tests:**
  - 574 unit tests in 74 suites.
  - The smoke test has 76 checks. The new ones:
    - a cart needs a customer token, and a new customer has an empty one;
    - the cart refuses an unknown pack size (`404`) and more than 4 packs (`400`);
    - a pack in stock goes into one customer's cart, with today's price and no IDs;
    - a second customer's cart stays empty, and removing a pack or emptying the cart answers `204`.

    Without a pack in stock, the check that adds one has nothing to try and passes, so the count stays 76 on an empty shop.
  - **By hand, on 2026-11-06:**
    - an Admin made a live product with two pack sizes in stock;
    - a new customer signed up, put 3 packs of one size and 1 of the other in the cart (4 packs, ₹2,146, MRP ₹2,400), and was refused:
      - a 5th pack in the other size (`409`);
      - 5 packs at once (`400`);
      - an unknown SKU (`404`);

      the Admin's token on the cart got `401`;
    - moving the packs between sizes worked while the total stayed at 4;
    - the smoke test ran while stock was in the shop;
    - the Admin lowered a price, and sold out the other size. The cart showed the new price at once, and the sold-out line as `OUT_OF_STOCK`, outside the totals. Its packs could go down but not up;
    - the Admin took a pack off sale. Its line showed `OFF_SALE`: it could no longer be changed (`404`), only removed;
    - the customer emptied the cart. A Super User wrote the stock off and deleted the product and the category.

    The database held no carts afterwards.
- **Lessons** (ADR 0003):
  - **A trigger that runs after the row is written locks the parent with `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`.** The row's own foreign key has already taken a lock that `FOR UPDATE` would wait for. The image triggers of part 4D run before the row is written, so `FOR UPDATE` is right there.
  - **Make or find a row and lock it in one statement,** with `INSERT … ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE … RETURNING`.
  - **A smoke check that needs data passes trivially without it,** so the number of checks never depends on what is in the shop.
- **Still open:**
  - checkout and orders: fixing the price, holding the stock (from the batch that expires first), delivery charges, and GST in whole paise;
  - sending a guest's picks after sign-in, which is the apps' and the website's job: one `PUT` for each pack size;
  - a different limit for some products, if the owner wants one later;
  - a customer's cart shown to staff for support, which the permission matrix doesn't ask for.
